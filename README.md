# AEM 6991 — MPS Capstone Project I

Course site for AEM 6991 (Charles H. Dyson School of Applied Economics
and Management, Cornell University), taught by
[Ariel Ortiz-Bobea](https://arielortizbobea.github.io/).

**Live site: <https://arielortizbobea.github.io/aem6991/>**

- `fall-2026/` — the Fall 2026 offering: syllabus, schedule, and one page
  per meeting. Offerings are archived by year; a past year's folder is
  never edited.
- `fall-2026/_schedule.yml` is the single source of truth for the
  schedule. The front-page table, the syllabus table, the sidebar, and
  the meeting pages are all generated from it, so they cannot disagree.
- Built with [Quarto](https://quarto.org).
- Produced with AI assistance, disclosed per the course's own policy;
  the instructor wrote and verified every claim.

Quiz items, the roster, and student work are not in this repository;
students reach what they need through Canvas.
